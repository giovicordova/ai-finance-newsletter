### Pattern context
Recent editions tracked how the AI build-out is financed and who supplies the silicon: chip-backed borrowing (SpaceX, Anthropic's credit package), labs' own economics (Anthropic's IPO prep, 14 Oct), and hyperscalers industrialising in-house inference chips (Microsoft Maia 300, 13 Oct). Today adds a new financing actor — a foreign government. Japan is bankrolling up to $21 billion of U.S. chip-manufacturing capacity that GlobalFoundries will operate, with the U.S. Commerce Department taking an equity stake. The through-line of 2026 — that AI demand is pulling in ever-larger and more unusual pools of capital (private credit, foreign-state money, government equity) to fund the compute underneath it — tightens again.
