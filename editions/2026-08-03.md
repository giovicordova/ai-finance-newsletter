### Pattern context
Today's beat continues the dominant thread of the past two weeks: the AI build-out is showing up first in supplier economics. SK hynix posted record Q2 results on Wednesday (79.3T won revenue, 76% operating margin, HBM4 shipments); Celestica raised its outlook on data-centre hardware demand; Amazon Web Services accelerated to an 18-quarter-high growth rate. Kioxia is the NAND-flash (storage-memory) leg of the same story — the same AI-data-centre demand that lifted high-bandwidth memory is now lifting flash. Last week's weekly take framed it as suppliers posting records while buyers (Meta, Amazon) foot the bill with collapsing free cash flow; Kioxia sits squarely on the supplier side. The counter-thread to watch this week: whether Palantir (Monday) and AMD (Tuesday) extend the demand-confirmation into the software and merchant-silicon layers, and whether the Bloomberg ~$2.6T commitments figure reframes the financing-strain question the channel has tracked since Meta's cash-flow print.
