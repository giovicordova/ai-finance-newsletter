### Pattern context
The AI-capital-markets story that has run all month reached its public-market test: after Anthropic priced the largest IPO ever at $83 (19 Nov edition), the stock's first session opened 34% higher, faded, and still closed up 17% at $97.17 — a strong but softening verdict, delivered on a day the broader indices fell. It caps a week of record hard numbers (Cisco 16 Nov, Nvidia 18 Nov) and tightening policy (the chip-export rule, 17 Nov). The recurring thread — the same seven chip/cloud names (Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Microsoft, Micron, TSMC) recurring as suppliers, financiers and now Anthropic shareholders — is the spine of today's Friday weekly take.
