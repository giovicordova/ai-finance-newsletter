# AI Finance Briefing — 18 August 2026

## Telegram — News Post

Nvidia agreed to guarantee up to $105 billion of OpenAI's lease and power payments for a data centre in Pike County, Ohio. SoftBank's SB Energy will build and own the roughly 8-gigawatt site, then lease it to OpenAI for 20 years. The site will run only Nvidia chips.

### Pattern context
This extends the dominant August thread — who pays for the AI buildout, and how the cost lands on the income statement. Last week reframed it from demand to financing: Nvidia lined up over $500 billion of third-party capital (10 Aug) and the neocloud prints (CoreWeave, Nebius) showed the debt and capex hitting the books. Today's beat is the same thread in concrete form: rather than customers borrowing to buy compute, Nvidia itself now backstops a single customer's 20-year lease and power bills to the tune of $105 billion — vendor financing at unprecedented scale, tying the chipmaker's revenue directly to OpenAI's ability to fill an 8-gigawatt campus. Kept strictly to the verifiable structure (guarantee amount, capacity, lease term, owner) with no commentary on the circularity, which belongs in a Friday take, not a Tuesday news post.
