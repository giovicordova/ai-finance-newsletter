### Pattern context
This week's spine was "who pays for the AI buildout, at what valuation, and is the demand real." Mon–Thu ran the capital side (OpenAI's scaled-back $11B/$1.25T raise, 9 Nov), the physical-constraint side (Anthropic's $3.4B gas-turbine order, 10 Nov), the valuation peak (Anthropic's $70–$78 IPO pricing at up to ~$1.4T, 11 Nov) and the demand proof (CoreWeave's near-tripled revenue and ~$133B backlog, 12 Nov). Today's beat is a different kind of risk entirely: not whether demand is real, but how fragile the keystone is — Jensen Huang, the single most important executive in the AI hardware chain, out effective immediately, days before Nvidia's ~17 Nov earnings. It caps a week that moved the market's worry from "is AI overvalued?" to "how concentrated and reversible is this whole structure?" — the same handful of names (Nvidia, OpenAI, Anthropic, the hyperscalers) are the buyers, builders and financiers, and now the most central of them is leaderless.

### Weekly threads (Friday only)
- **Demand is real, and large.** CoreWeave's Q3 revenue nearly tripled to $3.66B with a ~$133B backlog ([12 Nov](https://github.com/giovicordova/ai-finance-newsletter/blob/main/editions/2026-11-12.md)); Anthropic set an IPO price implying up to ~$1.4T ([11 Nov](https://github.com/giovicordova/ai-finance-newsletter/blob/main/editions/2026-11-11.md)). - **Capital and power are both getting expensive to secure.** OpenAI's round shrank from $30B+/$1.4T to ~$11B/$1.25T as the rout forced terms down ([9 Nov](https://github.com/giovicordova/ai-finance-newsletter/blob/main/editions/2026-11-09.md)); Anthropic spent $3.4B buying gas turbines directly after a supplier couldn't finance them ([10 Nov](https://github.com/giovicordova/ai-finance-newsletter/blob/main/editions/2026-11-10.md)). The labs increasingly hold the capital, and the bottleneck keeps moving down the stack — from chips to money to megawatts.
- **Concentration and key-man risk, made concrete.** The buyers, suppliers and financiers of AI are the same few names, and this week the most central of them — Nvidia — lost its CEO overnight ([13 Nov](https://github.com/giovicordova/ai-finance-newsletter/blob/main/editions/2026-11-13.md)), underlining how much of a multi-trillion-dollar cluster of valuations rests on a very short list of people and companies.
