### Pattern context
This week's through-line stays on the AI-chip supply map and how violently it is repricing. 5 Oct: Broadcom's up-to-$42B loan to Anthropic (supplier-as-lender). 6 Oct: Cerebras shares swinging 9–20% in a week on which OpenAI workload lands on which chip. 7 Oct: Etched — a private Nvidia challenger building inference-only chips — drawing offers at double its two-month-old $21B valuation, itself already quadruple its June $5B mark. The recurring names are the Nvidia alternatives (Cerebras, Broadcom, AMD, and now Etched) and the demand pulling them up (OpenAI, Anthropic). The thread to watch is how fast private chip valuations are compounding — Etched has roughly 8x'd since June — and whether that pace holds once real deployment numbers, not just offers, arrive.
