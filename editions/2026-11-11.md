### Pattern context
This week's spine has been "who pays for the AI buildout" and at what valuation: Mon–Thu ran the supply side (AMD, Arm, Microchip chip earnings) and the capital side (OpenAI's scaled-back $11B/$1.25T raise on 9 Nov, Anthropic's $3.4B gas-turbine order on 10 Nov). Today's beat is the capital story reaching its loudest test — Anthropic putting a concrete $70–$78 range and a ~$1.4T price tag on the largest AI IPO yet, days after the 10 Nov edition could only flag the listing as a rumor. The $26B of chipmaker/hyperscaler purchases inside the filing echoes the recurring "AI's funders are also its customers and suppliers" thread. The listing prices into a week of AI-stock weakness (Nvidia's slide), which is exactly why the debut is being framed as a validation test — but that framing is directional and stays out of the news post.
