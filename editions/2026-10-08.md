### Pattern context
This week's through-line stays on how the AI compute buildout is being *financed* — and increasingly on debt. 5 Oct: Broadcom's up-to-$42B loan package to Anthropic (supplier-as-lender). 6 Oct: Cerebras shares swinging on which OpenAI workload lands where. 7 Oct: Etched drawing offers at double its two-month-old $21B valuation. Today: SpaceX lining up ~$40B of borrowing — collateralised by the GPUs themselves — to buy Nvidia chips, via the same Apollo-led lending machinery Nvidia stood up in August. The recurring names are Nvidia (as both seller and financing backstop) and the private-credit giants (Apollo, Blackstone, KKR) now underwriting the chip orders. The thread to watch: GPU-collateralised debt is becoming the default funding tool, and it assumes the chips hold their value (~7 years, per CNBC's credit source) across a buildout whose demand is still unproven.
