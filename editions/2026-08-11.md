### Pattern context
The channel's dominant August thread has been financing the AI buildout, not whether demand is real. Today's Nvidia platform is that thread's clearest confirmation yet: the chipmaker itself is now organising Wall Street capital so customers can borrow against compute. It also reframes tonight's CoreWeave and Super Micro prints — both AI-infrastructure names carrying heavy spending and debt.
