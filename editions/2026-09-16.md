### Stories considered
- **Cornelis Networks raises $205M to challenge Nvidia in AI networking (CHOSEN).** Cornelis Networks, an Intel spinout (the former Omni-Path team, independent since 2020), announced a $205 million round led by IAG Capital Partners on 14 Sep, alongside a new product called Active Compute Fabric. The company builds the networking layer that connects AI accelerators, and its pitch is an open architecture that works with any vendor's GPUs rather than a single supplier's stack — a direct challenge to Nvidia's networking dominance. Chosen as the freshest (Sun→Mon), cleanly money-moving AI-infrastructure beat with a genuine primary-quality allowlisted free source (TechCrunch) that carries every claim in the post, and a distinct thread from the week's three-day AI-slowdown story. https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/14/ai-infrastructure-company-cornelis-raises-205m-to-chip-away-at-nvidias-dominance/
- **Firmus targets ~US$5B ASX IPO (runner-up, verifiability).** Nvidia-backed Australian AI data-centre builder Firmus is reportedly taking a roadshow to Asia this week for an ASX listing targeted at end-October that could raise as much as US$5 billion (~A$7 billion) — potentially one of Australia's largest floats — with OpenAI as anchor customer and contracted capacity above 900MW. Bigger money than Cornelis, but the fresh IPO-launch figures ($5B, October, valuation) traced primarily to Bloomberg (paywalled) and Capital Brief; the allowlisted free Yahoo Finance coverage only confirms the earlier facts (900MW, OpenAI anchor, ~$10.5B last-round valuation), not the launch.
