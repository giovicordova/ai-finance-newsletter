### Pattern context
The running spine remains "who pays for the AI buildout, at what valuation, and is the demand real" — plus last week's fourth axis, "who is allowed to buy the compute." Today's beat is the keystone readout the whole week pointed at: Nvidia — the single most important supplier in the AI hardware chain — printed $115.8B in revenue (+103%), with $107.6B of it Data Center, and guided to $130B next quarter. After CoreWeave's near-tripled revenue (12 Nov), Cisco's record quarter and raised AI target (16 Nov) and the chip-export rule (17 Nov), this is the hardest, largest-scale demand confirmation yet — and the first since Jensen Huang's abrupt 12 Nov exit, so it doubles as the governance/key-man test the 13 Nov edition flagged. The demand side answered emphatically; the open questions the recent editions keep circling — concentration (three customers were ~36% of revenue), the China exclusion baked into guidance, and leadership continuity — are exactly what this week's remaining catalyst, Anthropic's IPO pricing (expected 18 Nov), and the eventual Friday synthesis will have to weigh.
