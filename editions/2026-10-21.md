### Pattern context
The week's dominant thread has been the capital mechanics of the AI buildout — Wall Street syndicating Broadcom's ~$60B debt for Anthropic's chips (20 Oct), TSMC's record quarter (16 Oct), GlobalFoundries' New York fabs (15 Oct), Microsoft's own Maia chips (13 Oct). Today's beat widens the lens geographically: Nvidia is now the one deploying capital, into Europe, with a national government co-investing in its chips. It extends the "suppliers and their financiers set the pace" pattern rather than breaking it — but shifts the stage from US compute financing to European sovereign-AI ambition.
