### Stories considered
- **Harvey raises $550M at $15.5B valuation (CHOSEN)** — Legal AI company Harvey announced (9 Sep) a $550 million round at a $15.5 billion valuation, co-led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Diffusion, with Sapphire Ventures and Whale Rock Capital Management joining alongside existing backers (Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, a16z, Coatue, GIC, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and others). ### Pattern context
This week's archive has been almost entirely about how the compute buildout is financed and supplied: Nscale's pre-IPO raise (7 Sep), Jane Street's $13B Crusoe compute lease (8 Sep), and the Qualcomm–Amazon custom-chip warrant (9 Sep). Harvey shifts the lens to the application layer — a legal AI company with $400M+ in recurring revenue commanding a $15.5B mark, and consolidating (Guardrails AI is its fourth 2026 acquisition). It rhymes with the Cognition AI raise noted yesterday: capital is now flowing to AI *applications* with real enterprise revenue, not only to the labs and the compute providers. Oracle's after-close earnings tonight will swing the thread back to infrastructure demand.
