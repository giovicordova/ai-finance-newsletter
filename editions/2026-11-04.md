### Pattern context
This continues the AI-build-out spine running through recent editions, now from the chip-supply side. The demand side has printed in sequence — Alphabet's Google Cloud +103% to $30.7B (29 Oct), Amazon Web Services +39% to $46.0B (30 Oct), Palantir's U.S. commercial revenue +148% overtaking its government business (3 Nov) — and the financing side kept stacking (Nscale's IPO prospectus and the $860M Nvidia lease guarantee, 2 Nov). AMD's record data-centre quarter (+97% to $8.5B, now ~two-thirds of revenue, with the Instinct MI450 ramping) is the cleanest evidence yet that AI-accelerator demand is broadening beyond Nvidia to a second credible supplier. The recurring tension — can real AI revenue grow fast enough to justify the financed buildout — now has a second chip seller scaling into it, even as AI-infrastructure shares sold off on 3 Nov.
