This week's spine has been "who pays for the AI buildout, at what valuation, and is the demand real": the capital side (OpenAI's scaled-back $11B/$1.25T raise, 9 Nov; Anthropic's $3.4B gas-turbine order, 10 Nov; Anthropic's $70–$78 IPO pricing at up to ~$1.4T, 11 Nov). That is the clearest hard-revenue readout on AI demand before Nvidia reports (~17–19 Nov). The $709M net loss and $37B–$39B capex also keep the week's other half in view — the demand is real, but so is the cost of serving it.
