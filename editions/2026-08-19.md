### Pattern context
For two weeks the briefing has run on the supply-and-financing side of the AI buildout — Nvidia's $500B third-party capital platforms (10 Aug), Anthropic's reported $11.5B quarter (17 Aug), and the $105B Nvidia guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio campus (18 Aug). Today's Baidu print is the demand-and-returns counterpoint: a large incumbent whose AI cloud line is compounding fast (infrastructure +50%, GPU cloud +283%) even as the legacy advertising engine shrinks, pulling total revenue down 4%. It is the first edition this cycle where AI revenue and legacy revenue are visibly trading places inside a single reporting company — the crossover the capex thread has been implicitly betting on. Kept strictly to the filed figures; the "is AI growth outrunning the old business fast enough" question is Friday-take territory, not a Wednesday news post.
