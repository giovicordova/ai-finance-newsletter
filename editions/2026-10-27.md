### Pattern context
The month's spine has been the capital mechanics of the AI buildout — the spending side (Meta's raised ~$135–145B capex outlook, 22 Oct; Nokia's +123% AI/cloud sales, 23 Oct) and the funding side (Wall Street syndicating Broadcom's ~$60B debt for Anthropic's chips, 20 Oct; SpaceX's $40B GPU debt, 26 Oct; the neocloud credit stress of 22–24 Oct). Anthropic's public S-1 is the clearest single document yet tying those threads together: a company with a ~14x revenue jump to $16.3B in six months, a headline loss that is almost entirely non-cash, over $112B of cash on hand, and hundreds of billions in non-cancelable compute commitments behind it — now asking public markets to fund the rest. It lands two days before Microsoft and Alphabet report (Wed) and three before Amazon (Thu), which will test whether the hyperscalers keep guiding capex higher into the same tightening-credit backdrop.
