### Pattern context
This week's beat has centred on the AI capital cycle: Alibaba's 75% capex jump and $10.2B Hong Kong share sale (Aug 21, 24), SpaceXAI adopting Nvidia's Vera chip (Aug 25), Baidu's AI-cloud growth (Aug 19). Nvidia's print sits at the supply side of that same story — the vendor whose revenue is the other companies' spending. Its data-centre line is effectively a live readout of how much the hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) and neoclouds are still buying. A soft number would land against a backdrop where regulators (Bank of England, the Fed minutes) have begun naming AI concentration as a stability question.
