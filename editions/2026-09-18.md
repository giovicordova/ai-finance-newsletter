### Pattern context
The week ran on one spine with three strands. Monday–Tuesday priced the fallout from Dario Amodei's weekend "slow down" essay as a rotation — chipmakers down, cybersecurity up. Wednesday the Federal Reserve delivered its first rate hike in three years (target 3.75%–4.00%), a macro overhang on every capex-heavy AI name. Thursday reversed most of the damage: chips led a broad rebound as yields fell. Running underneath the whole week, capital kept flowing to the top of the stack — OpenAI floated near $1.2T (up from $852B in March), Cornelis Networks' $205M raise for open, anti-Nvidia networking, and the Firmus IPO roadshow. Today's beat closes the loop: the caution rhetoric peaked (Amodei, then a literal king), and the market answered by buying the dip.
