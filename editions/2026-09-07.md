## Telegram — News Post

Nscale, a two-year-old AI cloud company, is in talks to raise up to $3.5 billion before going public. About $2 billion would come from Nvidia. The rest is a $1.5 billion loan that can convert into shares, led by hedge fund Third Point. It values Nscale near $30 billion.

## Notes (archive only — not sent to Telegram)

### Stories considered
- **Nscale seeking up to $3.5B in pre-IPO financing (CHOSEN)** — London-based AI cloud/compute provider Nscale, founded ~2 years ago, is in talks to raise as much as $3.5 billion ahead of a planned US IPO (as early as this month). Structure: up to $1.5 billion of convertible notes led by Daniel Loeb's Third Point, plus about $2 billion in financing from Nvidia; the convertible notes are capped at a ~$30 billion valuation (roughly double the ~$14.6B March 2026 mark). Goldman Sachs is advising. Chosen as the freshest, cleanest AI-finance beat: it moves the story from chips/earnings to how the compute buildout is actually being financed, and extends the Anthropic $45B compute-lease thread. Corroborated across TechCrunch, Bloomberg (paywalled — not linked), Yahoo Finance, TheNextWeb and CryptoBriefing; linked to TechCrunch (allowlisted, free). https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/ai-compute-provider-nscale-is-looking-for-3-5b-in-pre-ipo-financing/
- **Nscale ~$103B contracted revenue backlog (runner-up, related).** Nscale reportedly told investors its contracted revenue backlog nearly doubled to ~$103B from ~$51B a month earlier, driven by a $45B Anthropic compute lease (West Virginia campus) that Microsoft and Google passed on. A striking figure, but it is a projected backlog from signed leases, not sales — harder to state cleanly and verifiably in a news post without directional colour, so held for Notes. https://cryptobriefing.com/nscale-103-billion-contracted-revenue-ipo/
- **Meta's $600B US investment pledge (runner-up).** Mark Zuckerberg told the White House Meta will invest "at least $600 billion" in US infrastructure, jobs and AI data centres through 2028. Big number but largely a restatement of prior guidance and framed around a political dinner rather than a fresh, cleanly dated financial event; not picked. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-plans-600-billion-us-175901659.html
- **ACI Worldwide to acquire Cranium Ventures (runner-up).** Payments firm ACI Worldwide agreed to buy cloud-native card-switching developer Cranium Ventures; a real fintech M&A item but small and only loosely AI-anchored.
- **Data-centre spend to reach $31.6T by 2050 (PwC, via Bloomberg, runner-up).** Long-horizon forecast, not a 24-hour beat; useful context only.
