### Stories considered
- **Meta Platforms Q2 2026 results — chosen for the news post.** Released after the US close on Wednesday 29 July 2026 via PR Newswire (press-release wire, allowlisted, free); the official release is also on Meta's investor site (investor.atmeta.com). Revenue rose 28% year-over-year to $60.80 billion, on a 14% rise in ad impressions and a 12% rise in average price per ad. Net income was $15.8 billion, down about 14%; operating margin fell to 31% from 43% a year earlier as total expenses climbed ~55% (including a ~$2.4 billion legal charge). Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, were $31.08 billion — spending on servers, data centres and network infrastructure. Free cash flow collapsed ~91% to $784 million, and Meta funded part of the build-out with new long-term debt (long-term debt totalled ~$83.7 billion at 30 June). It narrowed full-year 2026 capital-spending guidance to $130–145 billion (from $125–145 billion) and guided Q3 revenue to $61–64 billion. **This is the single most important AI-finance beat of the window** — the sharpest read yet on this week's live thread, the financing of the AI build-out: a hugely profitable advertising company saw its free cash flow nearly wiped out by AI infrastructure spending in a single quarter. The news post links the PR Newswire primary and sticks to three verifiable headline facts (the $60.8B revenue and +28% growth, the $31B quarterly capital spending, and the ~91% free-cash-flow drop to $784M). The margin compression, the legal charge, the debt total, and the guidance ranges are held in these Notes to keep the post to headline facts. [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meta-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302838214.html]
- **Microsoft fiscal Q4 2026 results; Azure crosses $100 billion in annual revenue — runner-up, strong but a growth milestone rather than the week's key tension.** Reported after the close on 29 July 2026. Quarterly revenue was ~$90 billion with earnings per share of $4.74, both above forecast; the Intelligent Cloud segment posted $39.31 billion (+31.6%), and Azure grew 43% in the quarter, taking full-year Azure revenue past $100 billion for the first time (+41%). Microsoft also booked a $3.2 billion gain on its stake in the AI lab Anthropic. A very strong print, but it confirms the demand/growth side the channel has already tracked; Meta's report is the newer, more consequential financing read, so Microsoft is held as the runner-up. [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/microsoft-msft-q4-earnings-report-2026.html]

### Pattern context
The channel has run the AI build-out story for over two weeks: financing (Monday's NAVER/NVIDIA/Brookfield up-to-$10B Korean data-centre plan), hardware demand (Tuesday's Celestica Q2, revenue +62%), and memory (Wednesday's record SK hynix print). It pairs with Microsoft's same-day print, where Azure crossed $100 billion in annual revenue — the demand side still accelerating even as the cash cost of supplying it climbs.
