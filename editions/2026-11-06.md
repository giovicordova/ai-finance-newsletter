### Pattern context
The 5 Nov print closes the week's AI-build-out spine from the interconnect layer. The demand side printed in sequence through late October and this week — Alphabet's Google Cloud +103% to $30.7B (29 Oct), Amazon Web Services +39% to $46.0B (30 Oct), Palantir's U.S. commercial revenue +148% overtaking its government business (3 Nov) — and the chip-supply side stacked: AMD's data-centre revenue +97% to a record $8.5B (4 Nov), Arm's data-centre royalty revenue roughly doubling (5 Nov), and now Microchip's PCIe Gen6 data-centre connectivity design wins doubling 12→24 in a single quarter. The recurring tension — can real AI revenue grow fast enough to justify the financed buildout — now has corroboration across cloud, enterprise software, accelerators, chip IP, and interconnects. Yet AI-infrastructure shares fell most of the week (Nvidia ~13.6% below its October high, Burry-disclosed shorts, a broad 5 Nov selloff with AMD −13% days after a record quarter). The divergence between stacking fundamentals and falling prices is the week's defining feature and the spine of today's weekly take.
