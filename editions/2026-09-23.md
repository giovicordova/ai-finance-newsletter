### Stories considered
- **Alibaba unveils Zhenwu V900, "China's most powerful" AI chip (CHOSEN).** At its annual Apsara Conference in Hangzhou on Tuesday (22 Sep), Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu unveiled the Zhenwu V900 accelerator from its T-Head chip unit, describing it as China's most powerful AI chip. It triples the performance of the Zhenwu M890 launched in May, can be combined in clusters of up to 500,000 units to train frontier models, and is designed to rival Nvidia's processors, which tightening US export controls largely keep out of China. Alibaba also outlined plans to expand Alibaba Cloud's data-centre capacity to more than 20 gigawatts by 2032 (backed by a >$53bn three-year AI spend) and teased a model up to four times larger than its current flagship Qwen. Shares rose (Hong Kong close ~2%, US premarket ~3%).
