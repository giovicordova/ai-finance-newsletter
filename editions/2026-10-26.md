### Pattern context
The debt behind the AI buildout is now the dominant thread. Last week's editions tracked the spending side — Meta's raised capex (Oct 22) and Nokia's 123% jump in AI/cloud sales (Oct 23) — while this week the strain moved to how it's funded: CoreWeave credit stress (Oct 15), the neocloud sell-off (Oct 22-24), and Anthropic's Blackstone package all point the same way. SpaceX's $40bn is the largest single financing in that arc, and notably Musk framed it as investment-grade and warned that most neoclouds will fail — the buildout consolidating around a few balance-sheet-strong buyers. Big-tech earnings Wed/Thu will test whether the spenders keep guiding capex higher into that tightening credit backdrop.
