### Stories considered
- **Nvidia board reinstates Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director (SEC Form 8-K filed 23 Nov 2026) [CHOSEN].** Eleven days after co-founder Jensen Huang resigned on 12 Nov, Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate him as president, chief executive and a director, effective immediately — reversing the position Chairman Harvey Jones stated on 17 Nov that the board "does not intend to reinstate" him. The filing says the board became satisfied with his candor and ability to lead after a series of discussions and meetings, and that, based on input from senior levels of the US government, his reinstatement poses no national security risk. The company's independent investigation into Huang will proceed to conclusion, and Huang is committed to cooperating; Colette Kress, who had served as interim CEO, returns to her role as chief financial officer. Chosen: this is the freshest, largest-scale and most verifiable AI-finance event in the 24h window — a keystone corporate-governance reversal at the single most important supplier in the AI hardware chain, resolving (for now) the leadership-continuity question the 13 Nov and 18 Nov editions flagged, sourced to Nvidia's own primary SEC filing. The news post keeps strictly to the cleanest verifiable facts carried by the 8-K — the reinstatement as president and chief executive effective immediately, the unanimous board vote, the national-security clearance based on senior-US-government input, and the ongoing independent investigation. It omits the ~31% share jump (directional, barred from the news post), the personal-conduct and export-control/smuggling backdrop (not the finance beat and partly secondary-sourced), the 1–2 Dec House committee testimony, and the Kress-to-CFO detail (secondary, for char budget).
