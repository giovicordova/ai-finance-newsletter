# AI Finance Briefing — 1 October 2026

## Telegram — News Post

Micron reported record quarterly revenue of $54.2 billion, up 31% from the prior quarter, on demand for memory chips in AI servers. Its main memory line, DRAM, brought in $39.8 billion. Most of its 2027 AI-memory supply is already sold. It guided to ~$61.5 billion next quarter.

### Stories considered
- **Micron record fiscal Q4 2026 results; strong fiscal 2027 outlook (CHOSEN).** Reported after the close Tuesday 30 Sep — fresh for this morning's beat and the candidate flagged in yesterday's edition. Micron posted record quarterly revenue of $54.23 billion (vs. ~$51B consensus), up 31% sequentially, with non-GAAP EPS of $33.42 (vs. ~$31.6 consensus) and a Q4 gross margin near 87%. DRAM revenue hit $39.8 billion (up 343% year-over-year, ~73% of sales); data-center SSD revenue was around $10 billion. Full-year FY2026 revenue was a record ~$133.2 billion. Micron said most of its 2027 high-bandwidth memory (HBM) supply is already contracted and guided fiscal Q1 2027 to ~$61.5 billion (±$1.5B) revenue and ~$38.15 EPS, with tighter memory supply-demand expected through fiscal 2027–2028. Chosen: a fresh, money-moving, primary-sourced corporate earnings event that puts a hard number on AI-driven memory demand — a new angle on the week's AI-capital thread (so far chips, buybacks, and the OpenAI mega-raise, not the memory suppliers). The post links the filed press release and states only figures that release supports; the "numbers don't look real" / "largest ever" framing is kept out. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm

### Pattern context
The past three weeks ran on AI capital intensity from the top of the stack down: the chip buildout (Advanced Micro Devices past $1 trillion), Nvidia's record $150B buyback increase (29 Sep), Anthropic's $11.6B Akamai compute deal (28 Sep), and OpenAI's reported ≥$30B raise at a ~$1.4T valuation (30 Sep). Today's beat drops a rung to the component layer that actually feeds those data centres: Micron, the memory supplier, posted record revenue with DRAM up 343% year-over-year and most of its 2027 AI-memory supply already sold. It's the clearest hard evidence yet that the capex the week has been tracking is landing as real, booked demand — and the "most of 2027 already contracted" line is the thread to watch into next year.
