# AI Finance Briefing — 28 July 2026

## Telegram — News Post

Celestica, which builds servers and networking gear for data centres, reported second-quarter revenue of $4.7 billion, up 62% from a year earlier. It raised its full-year 2026 revenue forecast to $20.5 billion, from $19 billion, citing demand from data-centre customers.

## Notes (archive only — not sent to Telegram)

### Stories considered
- **Celestica Q2 2026 earnings and raised full-year outlook — chosen for the news post.** Released after the close on Monday 27 July 2026 via GlobeNewswire (press-release wire, allowlisted, free); conference call held Tuesday 28 July at 8:00 a.m. ET. Revenue rose 62% year-over-year to $4.70 billion (from $2.89 billion). GAAP earnings per share were $3.17 (from $1.82); adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings per share were $2.54 (from $1.39). Both revenue and adjusted profit came in above the high end of prior guidance. The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to $20.5 billion, up from $19.0 billion, and expects growth to accelerate into 2027. The gain was led by data-centre demand: the Connectivity & Cloud Solutions segment grew ~84% to $3.81 billion and the Hardware Platform Solutions segment grew ~58% to roughly $1.9 billion. **This is the single most important AI-finance beat of the window** — a demand-side confirmation, with a raised forward forecast, that the AI-infrastructure build-out the channel has tracked (AMD Helios, Alphabet's $205B capex, NAVER/Brookfield's $10B) is translating into real hardware orders at a hyperscaler supplier. The news post links the GlobeNewswire primary and sticks to three verifiable facts (the $4.7B revenue and 62% growth, the raised $20.5B-from-$19B forecast, and the data-centre-demand attribution). The GAAP/adjusted EPS split, the segment breakdowns, and the 2027 acceleration comment are held in these Notes to keep the post to headline facts. [https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/27/3333904/0/en/Celestica-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results.html]

### Pattern context
For two weeks the channel has tracked the supply and financing of the AI build-out — AMD's Helios rack, Alphabet raising 2026 capital spending toward $205 billion, and the NAVER/NVIDIA/Brookfield $10 billion Korean data-centre plan. Today's Celestica print is the demand side of that same story: a contract manufacturer that assembles servers and networking gear for hyperscalers grew revenue 62% and lifted its full-year forecast, evidence the announced spending is turning into real hardware orders. It also opens a heavy earnings week (Microsoft and Meta on 29 July, Apple and Amazon on 30 July, SK Hynix on 29 July) that will test how far the demand extends up the stack.
