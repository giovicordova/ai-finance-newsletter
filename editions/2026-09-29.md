### Pattern context
The past two weeks ran on AI capital intensity: the two-power chip buildout (Advanced Micro Devices past $1 trillion, Alibaba's Nvidia rival), guardrail-free US-China AI talks, Anthropic's $11.6B Akamai compute deal, and rising financing strain (10-year Treasury yield at a 2007 high, Goldman's $1.2T-by-2027 capex forecast, SoftBank's record junk-bond raise for OpenAI). Today's beat flips the lens: the company at the centre of that spend, Nvidia, is now returning record cash to its own shareholders — a $150B buyback increase — while simultaneously committing ~$100B to OpenAI. The through-line holds: the AI infrastructure race is throwing off so much cash at the top of the stack that Nvidia can fund both the buildout and one of the largest buyback programs ever, even as the labs and telcos below it lean harder on debt.
