### Stories considered
- **FSB Chair Andrew Bailey warns G20 on frontier-AI cyber risk (CHOSEN)** — In a letter to G20 finance ministers and central bank governors ahead of their meetings this week (31 Aug–1 Sep, Asheville, North Carolina), Bailey (who also chairs the Financial Stability Board and governs the Bank of England) said the impact of frontier AI on cyber risk is the most immediate concern for the global financial system. Frontier AI "may have the ability materially to alter the speed, scale and economics of cyber risk, which could undermine market confidence system-wide, especially due to highly concentrated third-party service providers." He said many countries lack systems to manage safe deployment of advanced models, and called on authorities to support responsible model release and on financial institutions to strengthen response and recovery.
