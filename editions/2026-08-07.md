### Pattern context
Every weekday edition this week printed a fresh confirmation of AI-data-centre demand climbing the stack — Kioxia (NAND flash memory) Monday, Palantir (application software, +149% U.S. commercial) Tuesday, AMD (merchant silicon, data-centre revenue doubled) Wednesday — then Volta (a six-month-old "neocloud" borrowing $5 billion against a single $10 billion contract with chip-maker backing) Thursday shifted the lens to who is financing it. SoftBank closes the week on that same financing question from the other end: the biggest single AI backer posted a profit beat carried by its Intel stake while its OpenAI bet added nothing, even as its shares sit ~34% off a June high and lenders have balked at loans against its OpenAI stake. The operating results keep confirming demand; the market has stopped paying up for confirmation (AMD's ~8% drop on a double beat) and turned to the durability of the financing. Nvidia's own quarter, due late August, is the next scheduled test.
