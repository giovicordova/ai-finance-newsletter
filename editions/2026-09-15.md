### Stories considered
- **AI-slowdown warnings split the market: chips down, cybersecurity up (CHOSEN).** The weekend calls from Anthropic's Dario Amodei and OpenAI's Sam Altman to slow frontier AI development drove a sector split during Monday's US session. Chipmakers and AI-infrastructure names fell — Nvidia about 3%; Micron, Intel, Marvell Technology and Applied Materials more than 4% each; Hewlett Packard Enterprise about 11%; Dell 6%; Oracle 4%. Cybersecurity leaders rose the other way — Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike jumped more than 13% each, Zscaler ~15% — as investors bet on heavier spending to defend against AI-powered attacks. Chosen as the freshest, cleanest, market-moving AI-finance beat with a dedicated allowlisted source, and a genuinely new angle vs. Monday's post (which was the pre-open futures drop on the Amodei essay): the news here is the *rotation*, not the selloff. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/ai-stocks-slowdown-amodei-altman.html

Strong, honest update to Monday's beat, but the cleanest single close-recap source is the same CNBC live blog Monday's post already linked; the rotation story is fresher and has a distinct dedicated source.
