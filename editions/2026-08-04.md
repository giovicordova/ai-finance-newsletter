### Pattern context
For two weeks the channel's AI build-out story has lived on the supply side — memory (SK hynix, Kioxia), contract manufacturing (Celestica), and cloud (Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure) all posting records off AI-data-centre demand. Palantir is the first big print of this window from the software/applications layer: the company that sells AI tools to enterprises and governments, rather than the chips and servers underneath them. Its 149% U.S. commercial growth and raised ~$8.15B full-year forecast say the demand suppliers have been citing is now converting into application-software revenue too. The counter-read to watch: AMD reports after Tuesday's close (the merchant-silicon leg), and the market's own doubt-then-rerate pattern from last week — where AI-spending worries flipped to enthusiasm on the megacap prints — is the backdrop against which Palantir's 12% after-hours move should be read.
