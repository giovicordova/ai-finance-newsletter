### Pattern context
The week's spine was the capital mechanics of the AI build-out, told from both sides: Wall Street syndicating Broadcom's ~$60B debt for Anthropic's chips (20 Oct), Nvidia's ~$10B Europe plan with a co-investing German government (21 Oct), and Meta quantifying the demand side with $34.9B of quarterly capital spending and a raised $135–145B full-year outlook (22 Oct). Meta's print was the hinge: it both sized the spending and, by scale, triggered Thursday's sell-off on doubts about returns. Friday's Nokia beat is the counter-note the news post sticks to — a supplier posting real, audited AI-driven revenue (+123% to AI & cloud customers, €1.5B new orders) even as the market questions whether the buyers' spending pays off.
