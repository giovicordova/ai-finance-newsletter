Purchases of property, equipment and intangibles — the money spent building out data centres and buying chips — reached $5.66 billion in the quarter, roughly ten times revenue. ### Pattern context
August's dominant thread has been how the AI buildout is financed and what it costs, not whether demand is real — Nvidia organising $500 billion of Wall Street capital (11 Aug) and CoreWeave's Q2 showing interest costs on its data-centre debt outrunning its losses (12 Aug). Nebius is the second hard neocloud print in two days and pushes the thread one notch further: demand keeps confirming (revenue up 454%, a $3 billion run rate, four contracts above $1 billion each), operating profit has actually turned positive, yet the company still spent $5.66 billion in a single quarter — about ten times its revenue — and stayed in the red on the bottom line. The buildout's revenue is arriving; the cash going out the door to chase it is arriving faster. Nvidia's own quarter, later in August, is the next test of the same thread.
