### Stories considered
- **Nvidia to invest $3.5B in MediaTek via convertible bonds (CHOSEN)** — Announced Monday 31 Aug; MediaTek shares rose ~10% on Tuesday 1 Sep. Nvidia takes the stake through bonds convertible into MediaTek shares. As part of the expanded partnership, MediaTek adopts Nvidia's NVLink Fusion (and newly announced NVHBM) interconnect technology, which lets hyperscalers, cloud providers and model developers design custom accelerator chips that connect into Nvidia's rack-scale data-centre systems. The deal also extends existing collaboration on RTX/DGX Spark PC chips and MediaTek's Dimensity Auto automotive platforms. In June MediaTek said its custom AI-chip business would bring in ~$2B of revenue this year, with a total addressable market up to ~$80B by 2027. Chosen because it is the freshest, most consequential AI-finance beat of the cycle, and it sits squarely on the week's two live threads at once: Nvidia investing across its own ecosystem, and the rise of custom silicon as a counterweight to Nvidia GPUs. ### Pattern context
This lands directly on threads the week has been building. On 27 Aug, Jensen Huang defended Nvidia's growing web of investments across the AI ecosystem; Friday's 28 Aug take noted every buildout record is "one firm's revenue and another's bill," increasingly financed by the chipmakers backing their own customers. The MediaTek stake is that pattern again — Nvidia putting capital into a partner — but it also answers the custom-silicon thread (Marvell's data-centre surge, 28 Aug): rather than fight in-house chips from Amazon, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic, Nvidia is selling the interconnect (NVLink Fusion) that all those custom chips plug into. Ceding the chip, keeping the scaffolding.
