### Pattern context
This cycle has been Nvidia-and-compute all week: Nvidia/MediaTek (2 Sep), Broadcom's AI print (3 Sep), Nvidia buying Hugging Face (4 Sep), and Nscale's pre-IPO financing with $2B from Nvidia (7 Sep). Today's beat keeps the compute-buildout thread but flips the vantage point from supply (providers raising capital) to demand (a quant trading firm signing a $13B compute contract). It is the first edition this cycle to feature a traditional finance firm as the buyer, and it stacks with Jane Street's existing ~$6B CoreWeave commitment — a sign the AI-infrastructure bill is now landing on Wall Street trading desks, not just AI labs. Both sides of this trade are still Bloomberg-reported and unconfirmed by the companies, so the post is framed as reported, not announced.
