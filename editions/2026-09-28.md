- **Akamai–Anthropic $11.6B, seven-year cloud deal (CHOSEN).** Announced Friday 25 Sep; carried through the weekend (Boston Globe, 27 Sep). Akamai's largest-ever contract (CEO Tom Leighton), covering Anthropic's accelerating CPU (non-GPU, "everyday") workload demands on Akamai Cloud's distributed infrastructure. Option to expand by ~$9B to ~$20B total; Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant vesting up to ~5% of its common stock; ~$5.5B total capex tied to the commitment and ~$1.7B added 2026 capex for supply (incl. memory). No change to 2026 revenue guidance. More than 6x a $1.8B May deal between the two. Today's beat rotates the lens back to the money underneath the buildout: not a government or a GPU order, but a hyperscaler-scale compute contract from a frontier lab (Anthropic) locking in distributed CPU capacity, with an equity warrant stapled on.
