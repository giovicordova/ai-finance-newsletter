### Pattern context
This week ran on the capital side of the AI buildout — Anthropic's reported $11.5B quarter and IPO groundwork (17 Aug), Nvidia's $105B guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio campus (18 Aug), Baidu's AI-cloud-vs-legacy crossover (19 Aug), and OpenAI's CFO naming a 2027 listing window (20 Aug). Alibaba is the same thread in its hardest form yet: a hyperscaler whose cloud line is accelerating (+45%) even as it triples down on capex (+75% to ~$10 billion), and whose profit fell 75% as a direct result. It is the clearest single-company view of the cost of the buildout arriving on the income statement — the demand-and-returns question the whole month's capex thread has been circling. Kept strictly to the filed figures; whether the spend outruns the returns is Friday-take territory, not a news-post line.
