### Pattern context
This week closed a clean loop: Monday's balance-sheet question (Alibaba diluting shareholders to fund AI) and Thursday's supply-side readout (Nvidia's record print) both point at the same circularity Huang defended on the call — the vendors are increasingly financing the customers whose orders become the vendors' revenue. Today's Marvell print rhymes with Nvidia's (records, accelerating data-centre growth, raised guidance) but adds the counter-thread: Big Tech's custom in-house silicon is scaling as a cheaper alternative to Nvidia, so the buildout is broadening beyond a single name even as it concentrates the money. The macro cap on the week is Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote (Friday, after this publishes) — the first Fed chair to speak into a bond market reshaped by ~$132B of hyperscaler AI-bond supply. Kept strictly to the release's figures; whether the circular financing is prudent or fragile is the take's job, not the news post's.
