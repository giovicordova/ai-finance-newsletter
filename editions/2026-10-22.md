### Pattern context
The week's spine has been the capital mechanics of the AI buildout, told from the supply side: Wall Street syndicating Broadcom's ~$60B debt for Anthropic's chips (20 Oct), Nvidia's ~$10B Europe plan (21 Oct), TSMC's record quarter (16 Oct), GlobalFoundries' New York fabs (15 Oct). Meta flips the camera to the demand side — the hyperscaler actually writing the cheques. A $34.9B quarterly capex run-rate and a raised $135–145B full-year outlook quantify what all that supplier financing is being built to serve, and extend the thread rather than break it.
