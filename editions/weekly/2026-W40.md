### Pattern context
The whole week ran on AI capital intensity, layer by layer: Anthropic's $11.6B Akamai compute deal (28 Sep), Nvidia's record $150B buyback increase (29 Sep), OpenAI's reported ≥$30B raise at a ~$1.4T valuation (30 Sep), and Micron's record $54.2B quarter with most of its 2027 memory already sold (1 Oct). Today's beat closes the loop on the demand/financing side: SoftBank finishing its $30B OpenAI commitment, now a ~13% owner, with the final payment funded by a record junk-bond sale. It directly extends Wednesday's OpenAI-raise beat and the recurring debt thread — the supply side's demand is now booked (Micron), while the capital filling it splits between self-funded cash at the top (Nvidia) and borrowed money below (SoftBank's bond-funded stake).
