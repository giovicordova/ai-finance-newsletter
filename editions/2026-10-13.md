### Pattern context
The newsletter's recent through-line has been how the AI compute buildout is financed and whether marks hold: Broadcom lending Anthropic ~$42B, SpaceX borrowing ~$40B against chips, Etched doubling to ~$40–50B, Firmus pulling its IPO (3 Oct week), and Monday's pivot to Nvidia possibly absorbing Reflection AI. Today turns to the demand side of that same capex: Microsoft — among the largest buyers of Nvidia's chips and a heavy spender on the buildout — is industrialising its own inference silicon (Maia 300) at multi-gigawatt scale for 2027. The recurring name Nvidia reappears, now as the incumbent the hyperscalers are trying to spend less with. The thread to watch is whether in-house chips from Microsoft (Maia), Amazon (Trainium) and Google (TPU) start to blunt the GPU-demand curve that has underwritten every private mark in this buildout.
