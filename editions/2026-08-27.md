### Pattern context
This closes the loop the week has been building toward. Monday-Tuesday framed the demand side (SpaceXAI adopting Nvidia's Vera chip, 25 Aug); Wednesday's edition previewed the print as the supply-side readout of hyperscaler spending; today is the number itself — data-centre revenue $89B, up 117%, well past the ~$86B the Street modelled, with a Q3 guide ($108B) above the ~$104B expected. It confirms rather than breaks the month's central thread: the AI capital cycle (Alibaba's capex jump and $10.2B raise, Big Tech tracking toward ~$650B of 2026 capex) is still accelerating on the vendor whose revenue is everyone else's spending. The one new wrinkle worth watching — Huang defending Nvidia's own financing of its customers as "low" risk — is exactly the circularity regulators (Bank of England, Fed minutes) have started naming, and is Friday-take material, not a Thursday news beat.
