### Pattern context
The past two-plus weeks ran on AI capital intensity: the two-power chip buildout (Advanced Micro Devices past $1 trillion, Alibaba's Nvidia rival), Anthropic's $11.6B Akamai compute deal (28 Sep), Nvidia's record $150B buyback increase (29 Sep), and the debt underneath it all (SoftBank's record junk-bond raise, the 10-year Treasury yield at a 2007 high). Today's beat moves up the stack to the demand side: the lab at the centre of the buildout is now reportedly seeking $30B at a $1.4T valuation, a bridge in place of a listing Altman has pushed off. It rhymes with the Nvidia–OpenAI ~$100B partnership — the same names, ever-larger sums, still mostly private-market capital chasing the compute race. The thread to watch: how much of this record demand is equity versus the debt that has been financing the supply side.
