### Pattern context
Last week's thread — the AI bill landing on the income statement — turned into the balance-sheet question over the weekend. Alibaba reported a 75% profit drop on a 75% capex jump (21 Aug); three days later it is issuing $10.2 billion of new equity, the largest new-share sale ever by a Hong Kong-listed company, to fund still more AI spending. That is the same story one step downstream: earnings absorbed the cost, and now the company is raising outside capital to keep the buildout going, diluting existing holders (the stock fell ~10%). It rhymes with the month's financing arc — Nvidia's $105B guarantee for OpenAI's Ohio campus (18 Aug) and OpenAI's 2027 IPO signal (20 Aug) — all different mechanisms for funding the same compute race. Kept strictly to the priced-deal figures; whether raising equity into a dilution-driven selloff is prudent or forced is Friday-take territory, not a Monday news post.
