### Pattern context
August's dominant thread has been how the AI buildout is financed, not whether demand is real — Volta's neocloud borrowing (06 Aug), SoftBank leaning on its Intel stake (07 Aug), and Nvidia organising $500 billion of Wall Street capital so customers can borrow against compute (11 Aug). CoreWeave is the first hard print to show both sides of that thread in one statement: revenue more than doubled to $2.58 billion and the backlog swelled past $104 billion, yet the net loss widened to $626 million because net interest expense — the cost of the debt funding its data centres — more than doubled to $640 million and now exceeds the loss itself. Demand keeps confirming; the financing cost of chasing it is now visible on the income statement. Nebius (12 Aug) and, later in the month, Nvidia's own quarter are the next tests.
